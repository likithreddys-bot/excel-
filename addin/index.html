    Privacy

    Strict (default): leave both boxes unticked. Only your question, column names, types, ranges and how empty each column is are sent. The AI then refers you to the result sheets instead of quoting numbers.

    Guardrails, always on: your files are read and calculated on this computer only and are never uploaded. Your rows, names, phone numbers, PAN, Aadhaar, e-mails, account numbers and ids are never sent: every request is checked before it leaves, and one that contains them is stopped. The page can talk to no server except Google's Gemini API. Nothing from your files is stored after you close the tab. See exactly what was sent with "What was sent to the AI".
    Training: on Google's free tier, Google may use what is sent (your question and column facts) to improve its products. On a paid (billing-enabled) key, Google's terms say prompts are not used to train its models. For real customer files, use a paid key from your company's Google Cloud account.
